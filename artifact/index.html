What we do

Three jobs, and we take all of them seriously.

A calendar that gets the department into the same room, a treasury that carries the costs, and somewhere to take a problem that is not your supervisor.

We are the route in

If something about your PhD is not working, there is somewhere to say so that is not your supervisor and not a form. Write to the board and it reaches whoever is best placed to act on it.

We make the calendar

A Science Lunch every month, the Lunch Lottery, the summer BBQ, the Pharm Apéro, the Career Lecture Series with the Postdoc Club, and a two and a half day retreat with the Biozentrum. Free pizza is a serious pedagogical instrument.

We hold the purse

Being represented costs you nothing, and so does almost everything we run: the Science Lunch feeds you, the Lunch Lottery raffles food vouchers, the Apéro and the BBQ are free. The hoodie you pay for yourself, at cost, and the retreat is ticketed and sponsored.

{{ groupCount }} research groups, one assembly

The assembly covers {{ groupCount }} research groups. Some send several delegates and some send one, so the person who understands your corridor may not be the one holding the portfolio you need.

What it is

Two institutes, one room, three days

The retreat began in 2009 as part of the Biozentrum PhD programme and is now run jointly with the Pharmazentrum, with more students from both departments joining every year. It is a two and a half day event of keynote lectures, PhD student talks, poster sessions and networking, and the discussions about new results and new methods are deliberately wrapped in a social programme, because that is what turns two large institutes into one community.

Everyone presents. That is the point of the thing rather than a condition attached to it: practising how you talk about your work, in front of people who do not already know it, is the skill the retreat exists to build.

Taking part

Every eligible student gets the link by email

Registration is not something you have to go looking for. Every PhD student eligible to attend is sent a registration link months ahead, whatever their focal area or year in the programme. Each participant is asked to present their own work, as a poster or a short talk, and receives 1 CP for a successful participation.
