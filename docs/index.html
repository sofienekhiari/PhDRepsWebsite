What we do

Three jobs, and we take all of them seriously.

Representation on the bodies that decide things, a calendar that gets the department into the same room, and a treasury that carries the costs.

We sit in the room

Two delegates hold the department deputy role, and the assembly keeps a named contact person for the Biozentrum, D-BSSE, the DBM, the Tropeninstitut and Chemistry. If something about your PhD is broken, there is a route for raising it that does not start with your supervisor.

We make the calendar

A Science Lunch every month, the Lunch Lottery, the summer BBQ, the Pharm Apéro, the Career Lecture Series with the Postdoc Club, and a two and a half day retreat with the Biozentrum. Free pizza is a serious pedagogical instrument.

We hold the purse

Being represented costs you nothing, and so does almost everything we run: the Science Lunch feeds you, the Lunch Lottery raffles food vouchers, the Apéro and the BBQ are free. The hoodie you pay for yourself, at cost, and the retreat is ticketed and sponsored.

The assembly

{{ groupCount }} research groups, one assembly

The assembly covers {{ groupCount }} research groups. Some send several delegates and some send one, so the person who understands your corridor may not be the one holding the portfolio you need.

How to reach us

Three ways in, none of them require a form.

1

Come to something

Almost everything we run is open and free. The easiest first step is a Science Lunch, where nobody will ask you to introduce yourself.

2

Write to your delegate

Find the person from your research group, or the one holding the portfolio your question belongs to. The board addresses are on the reps page.

3

Go to a deputy

Two delegates hold the department deputy role. That is the route for anything you would rather not raise yourself.
