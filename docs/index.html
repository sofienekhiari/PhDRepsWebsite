What we do

Three jobs, and we take all of them seriously.

Representation on the committees, a calendar that gets the department into the same room, and a treasury that pays for it — funded by the department and by sponsors.

We sit in the room

Two of us are department deputies, and more of us sit on the bodies where curricula, deadlines and doctoral regulations are decided. If something about your PhD is broken, we can raise it — by name, in the minutes, without your supervisor there.

We make the calendar

Science Lunch every month, the Lunch Lottery, the summer BBQ, the Pharm Apéro, the Career Lecture Series, and a two-day retreat that takes half a year to plan. Free pizza is a serious pedagogical instrument.

We hold the purse

Membership costs you nothing. The treasury pays for the pizza, the vouchers, the retreat subsidy and the hoodies, out of departmental support and sponsors who like standing in a room with two hundred future scientists.

The assembly

Nine research groups, one assembly

Every group in the department sends a delegate, so there is always someone who understands your corridor, your instrument queue and your supervisor.

How to reach us

Three ways in, none of them require a form.

1

Come to something

Every event is open and free. The easiest first step is a Science Lunch — nobody will ask you to introduce yourself.

2

Write to your delegate

Find the person from your research group, or the one holding the portfolio your question belongs to.

3

Go to a deputy

For anything you would rather not say out loud, the two department deputies can raise it without naming you.
